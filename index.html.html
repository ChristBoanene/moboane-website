Our Practices

Strategic Legal Counsel for Africa's Development

Mergers & Acquisitions

From due diligence to deal structuring and post-transaction integration, we guide clients through all phases of M&A transactions. Our approach emphasizes strategic value creation and risk mitigation. We have extensive experience advising on significant transactions across multiple sectors, including our current engagement on a transaction valued at FCFA 3 billion.

Energy

We counsel clients on power projects, renewable energy development, and energy transition initiatives. Our team has experience with solar, hydroelectric, and other renewable energy projects across Central Africa, supporting the continent's transition to sustainable energy sources.

Oil and Gas

Our oil and gas practice covers upstream, midstream, and downstream activities. We advise on production sharing contracts, joint ventures, regulatory compliance, and local content requirements in Cameroon's evolving energy sector and across the African continent.

Mining

We provide comprehensive legal advisory for mining projects, from exploration licenses to production agreements. Our expertise covers regulatory compliance, environmental regulations, community relations, and mining agreements in Cameroon and across the CEMAC region.

Infrastructure and Project Finance

We provide end-to-end legal support for infrastructure projects including transportation, utilities, and social infrastructure. Our project finance expertise includes PPP structures, financing arrangements, and risk allocation for major developments that transform African economies.

Telecommunications

We advise on telecommunications regulations, licensing, spectrum allocation, and digital infrastructure development. Our expertise supports the expansion of connectivity across Africa, enabling digital transformation and economic growth.

Sovereign Finance

We provide legal counsel to governments and public institutions on sovereign borrowing, international financial agreements, and development finance. Our work supports African nations in accessing capital markets and structuring transactions that fund critical national development projects.
